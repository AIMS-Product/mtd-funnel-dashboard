Counting rules

  • MTD uses Close meeting creation time in Pacific time.
  • Meeting title and the active Lane 2 title map determine booking channel; lead source stays separate.
  • Pearl’s Social DM bookings are shown separately from Lane 2.
  • Any non-canceled scheduled meeting starts or advances the 720-hour clock. A new qualifying credit is eligible only after more than 720 elapsed hours.
  • Canceled or declined meetings earn no credit and do not advance the clock. A qualifying no-show retains its credit; a reschedule does not earn another. A later qualifying replacement may earn credit under the clock rules.
